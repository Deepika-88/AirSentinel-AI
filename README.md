# 🌍 AirSentinel-AI

AI-powered environmental intelligence platform for air-quality monitoring,
pollution hotspot visualization, citizen observations, federated sensor
aggregation, explainable risk analysis, and environmental alerts.

---

## 📌 Problem

Air pollution can vary significantly across locations and time.

Traditional monitoring systems may not capture every local event, while
environmental information is often distributed across multiple data sources.

AirSentinel-AI demonstrates a unified platform that combines:

- Air-quality data
- Weather information
- Pollutant measurements
- Citizen observations
- Citizen photo evidence
- Distributed sensor-node insights
- Risk analysis
- Environmental alerts

---

## 💡 Solution

AirSentinel-AI combines environmental data into a single interactive
dashboard.
